- 2026-11-11 · 预印本 · 匹配：data visualization

Data visualization is increasingly understood as a political process shaped by creators' editorial design and data choices. Prior research has examined how such choices produce deceptive visualizations, focusing on social media users' misleading visualizations of public data. This overlooks how creators draw on professional expertise to produce propagandist visualizations of political narratives. We investigate visualization creators who design and share propagandist visualizations of political claims, such as immigration, criminality, and others, in US online discourse. ## p04 · Vibe Analysis: Exploring LLM Adoption by Data Visualization Practitioners

To fill this gap, we conducted semi-structured interviews with members of the Data Visualization Society, a global community of data visualization designers. ## p20 · Spaciousness and Style: Finding Old Rhetoric in Data Visualizations
- 2026-09-24 · Rhetoric Review · 匹配：data visualization

This article revisits Richard Weaver’s concept of spaciousness as a rhetorical effect and repositions it within contemporary visual rhetoric, particularly data visualizations. Although Weaver’s account of spaciousness is rooted in a political ideology that presumes shared cultural values, the concept offers a useful lens for understanding how style operates in visual communication. Drawing on the classical Ciceronian styles, this article argues that spaciousness functions as a visual analogue of the grand style, emphasizing abstraction rather than detail. Analyses of sparklines, cartographic maps, network visualizations, and metaphor-based displays show how plain style prioritizes instruction and transparency, grand style cultivates openness and shared meaning, and middle style modulates between visual scales through perceptual or interactive zooming. The article demonstrates how stylistic spaciousness in data visualization reinforces cultural assumptions about connectivity, complexity, and knowledge, offering a rhetorically grounded approach to studying visual style.
